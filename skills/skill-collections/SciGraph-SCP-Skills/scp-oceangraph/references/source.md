## Page content (readable extract)

🧪 The Historical Marine Biodiversity Knowledge Graph (OceanGraphRAG KG) is a domain-specific knowledge graph for historical marine biological records, developed by Gustavo Marcelo Núñez (noted on the page as an independent researcher) in 2026. Using Microsoft’s GraphRAG framework, it automates digitization/structuring of mid-20th-century South American (esp. Argentina) literature about marine mammals, seabirds, and resource utilization. The goal is to turn multi-page scanned/transcribed historical texts into a semantically queryable knowledge graph for natural-language retrieval and analysis.

### Tool list

- query_cypher — Execute any Cypher query statement, supporting flexible graph database operations
- get_kg_statistics — Obtain statistical information such as nodes, relationships, and type distribution of the knowledge graph
- get_entity_details — Obtain detailed information and relationships of entities based on entity identifiers
- get_experiment_workflow — Obtain the complete workflow of chemical experiments
