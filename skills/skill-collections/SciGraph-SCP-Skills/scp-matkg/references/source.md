## Page content (readable extract)

🧪 MatKG is a large-scale, autonomously generated knowledge graph in materials science. It extracts entities (materials, properties, applications, synthesis methods, etc.) and their relationships from over 5 million scientific papers using advanced NLP techniques.

Relationships are established based on statistical co-occurrence frequency, providing a structured repository to support materials discovery, recommendation systems, and analytics.

### Tool list

- query_cypher — Execute any Cypher query statement, supporting flexible graph database operations
- get_kg_statistics — Obtain statistical information such as nodes, relationships, and type distribution of the knowledge graph
- get_entity_details — Obtain detailed information and relationships of entities based on entity identifiers
- get_experiment_workflow — Obtain the complete workflow of chemical experiments
