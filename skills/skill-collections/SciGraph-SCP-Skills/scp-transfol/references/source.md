## Page content (readable extract)

🧪 TransFOL is a comprehensive multimodal knowledge graph designed for drug-drug interaction (DDI) prediction and complex relational reasoning. It integrates structured knowledge from four authoritative biomedical data sources (DrugBank, TWOSIDES, DrugCombDB, Phenomebrowser), covering entities such as drugs, proteins, genes, diseases, phenotypes, enzymes, and biological pathways. The knowledge graph supports deep learning-driven DDI prediction and complex logical query reasoning, with important applications in clinical drug safety assessment, drug repositioning, and precision medicine.

### Tool list

- query_cypher — Execute any Cypher query statement, supporting flexible graph database operations
- get_kg_statistics — Obtain statistical information such as nodes, relationships, and type distribution of the knowledge graph
- get_entity_details — Obtain detailed information and relationships of entities based on entity identifiers
- get_experiment_workflow — Obtain the complete workflow of chemical experiments
