## Page content (readable extract)

🧪 The Marine Expert Management Knowledge Graph is a domain-specific knowledge graph constructed to organize and integrate fragmented expert information in the marine science field. It captures heterogeneous data from multiple sources (official marine information websites, Baidu Encyclopedia, public reports, social media, scholarly literature) and transforms them into structured knowledge.

It supports management, discovery, and visualization of marine experts, their publications, research institutions, collaboration relationships, and research fields, for marine science think tanks and talent management systems.

### Tool list

- get_entity_details — Obtain detailed information and relationships of entities based on entity identifiers
